ENGAGEMENT RETROSPECTIVE
Client: Northwind Wholesale Distributors
Engagement: NetSuite Implementation (Multi-location inventory, EDI x2, Shopify x1)
Prepared by: Power Cloud Delivery Team
Date: 2026-02-05

SUMMARY
The Northwind engagement went live successfully on 2026-01-19, roughly three weeks
later than the original target date. Overall actual effort ran approximately 28%
over the original estimate. Below are the key drivers, useful for calibrating
future engagements with a similar profile (multi-location inventory + EDI with
retail partners).

WHAT DROVE OVERRUNS
1. EDI partner certification was the single largest schedule risk, not NetSuite
   configuration itself. Partner-side certification testing added roughly 4 weeks
   beyond our internal integration build timeline, and this was largely outside
   our control. Task t034 (certification support) ran 3x over its original estimate.
2. Custom development for cross-location available-to-promise logic (t022) was
   underestimated. What looked like a standard configuration item in scoping
   turned out to require custom scripting once we understood the client's
   specific costing method.
3. Current-state process mapping (t002) and EDI requirements gathering (t003)
   both ran significantly over estimate during Discovery. In hindsight, clients
   with multiple legacy systems and manual reconciliation processes take longer
   to document accurately than clients with simpler current-state environments.
4. Hypercare (t061) ran 55% over estimate. Post go-live issue volume was higher
   than expected in week one, primarily related to inventory data migration
   discrepancies that weren't caught in UAT.

WHAT WENT WELL
- Shopify storefront integration (t032) and the ADP/Ramp finance syncs (t033)
  both came in at or under estimate. Standard ecommerce and finance-system
  connectors are comparatively low-risk and predictable.
- Role and permission setup (t023) also came in under estimate.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR SIMILAR FUTURE ENGAGEMENTS
- For clients with EDI relationships with major retail partners, add explicit
  schedule buffer for partner-side certification, and flag it as a risk item
  independent of internal team velocity.
- For available-to-promise or other cross-location logic, confirm early whether
  the client's costing method is standard NetSuite or will require custom
  scripting, and estimate accordingly rather than assuming standard configuration.
- Increase Discovery-phase effort estimates for clients with manual,
  spreadsheet-based current-state processes across multiple systems.
- Budget hypercare closer to actual historical average than the template default,
  particularly when data migration complexity is high.
